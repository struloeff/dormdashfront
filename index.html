Driving for DormDash

Drivers were Pepperdine students earning between classes, with flexible hours and weekly payouts.

  • $18–$25 / hour
    Most of each fare and 100% of tips went to the driver
  • Work between classes
    Claim jobs when you're free, ignore them when you're not
  • A real contract
    Every driver signed an independent-contractor agreement before their first job
