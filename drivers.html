Why Drive with DormDash?

Competitive Earnings

Drivers kept 82.68% of all delivery fees plus 100% of food costs. Average was $18-25/hour during peak times.

Fast Payouts

Drivers were paid automatically. New drivers received funds in 7-14 days, then every 2 business days.

Total Flexibility

No schedules, no bosses. Drivers worked whenever they wanted, around classes and other commitments.

Campus Community

Drivers delivered to fellow students on campus: short distances, familiar locations, and friendly customers.

How Driver Onboarding Worked

1

Get Approved

Drivers joined the DormDash driver community and were approved by an admin.

2

Create Account

Drivers signed up with their name and phone number, and the app verified they were approved.

3

Connect Bank

Drivers linked a bank account for automatic payouts. Setup was quick and secure.

4

Deliver & Earn

Orders came in through the driver app; drivers delivered to students and tracked their earnings.

Driver Requirements

Pepperdine Student - Had to be currently enrolled at Pepperdine University
Car & Valid Driver's License - Reliable vehicle with valid registration and license
Car Insurance - Active auto insurance policy was required
Smartphone - For receiving orders, navigation, and customer communication
Bank Account - To receive earnings via automatic payouts
